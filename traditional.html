Deep tech grows on this hill

Kumpula Campus is the largest concentration of natural sciences in the Nordic countries: 129 research groups across physics, chemistry, computer science, mathematics, geosciences and atmospheric research — with the Finnish Meteorological Institute as a neighbour and a track record of companies to show for it.
