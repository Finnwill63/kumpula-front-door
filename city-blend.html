Math & Inversion 47 core · 109 mapped
The mathematics that turns indirect measurements into images, models and certainty — the quiet backbone of the whole hill.
Atmosphere & Climate 41 core · 68 mapped
INAR and FMI side by side — aerosols, greenhouse gases and the measurement stations behind the numbers.
Artificial Intelligence 29 core · 86 mapped
Machine learning, data science and AI methods — from foundations to atmospheric and materials applications.
Resilience & Security 24 core · 67 mapped
Geosciences, cyber security and urban systems — the science of coping with a changing planet.

Quantum matter, computing and sensing — theory to cryostat.
