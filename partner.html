LIMITED · ASK

Straight answers

The questions every company actually has, answered on the page — not after three meetings.

Who owns the results?
Contract research: you do, per the agreement. Joint projects: agreed up front, project by project, before any work starts. Background IP always stays with its owner. No surprises after the fact.
What does it cost?
Contract work is priced per project at full cost — a scoping call gives you a written estimate before you commit anything. Co-funded projects typically carry 40–60%◦ public funding.
How fast is this, really?
Reply in two working days. Scoping inside two weeks. A measurement campaign in weeks; a joint project at funding-cycle pace. If we're not the right partner, we say so in the first call and tell you who is.
Do we need to be Finnish?
No. The campus works in English, and international partners use every format on this page.
