INAR · Institute for Atmospheric and Earth System Research

Atmospheric Aerosols Research Group

We study the physical properties of aerosol particles and the processes that modify their populations — new particle formation, growth, aerosol–cloud interactions — to reduce the uncertainties in climate and health impacts. If your question involves what's in the air and why, it probably passes through this hallway.

What we can do — in your language

Written for the visitor with a problem, not for the conference. Each capability links to the instruments and people behind it.

Measure

Particle formation & air quality measurement

Long-term and campaign measurements of aerosol formation, concentration and composition — outdoors, indoors, or at your site.

VIA · SMEAR STATIONS · MOBILE UNITS
Model

From measurement to prediction

Process models and machine learning that turn measurement series into forecasts — of new particle formation, urban air quality, or product behaviour.

VIA · MODELS + CSC COMPUTING
Advise

Instrumentation & method development

Two decades of building and calibrating aerosol instruments. If you're developing a sensor or need one validated, start here.
